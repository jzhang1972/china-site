Chongqing is unlike any other city in China. Known as the "8D Cyberpunk City," it's a place where monorails fly through buildings, streets are stacked on top of each other, and the night skyline looks like a scene from a sci-fi movie.

Viral videos have brought millions of tourists to this mountain city. But for a foreign traveler, Chongqing can also be confusing. Google Maps fails here, taxis can't find you, and cash is almost useless.

Here is your complete practical guide to surviving and enjoying Chongqing.

---

## 1. Before You Go: The Digital Essentials

Chongqing is a highly digital city. You need to prepare your apps **before** you arrive.

- [ ] **Set up Alipay**: Almost every shop, noodle stall, and taxi in Chongqing uses QR code payments. You cannot rely on cash here.
- [ ] **Get an eSIM**: You'll need reliable internet to use translation apps and navigation, as you'll be walking and climbing a lot.

## 2. Navigating the "8D City" (The Hardest Part)

In most cities, you walk on flat ground. In Chongqing, you might enter a building on the 1st floor and exit on the 15th floor onto a different street.

**The Golden Rule: Do not use Google Maps.** It cannot handle the multi-level streets.

- [ ] **Use Amap (高德地图)**: It's the only map that understands Chongqing's vertical layout.
- [ ] **Use Didi for Taxis**: Hailing a cab on the street is hard if you don't speak Chinese. Use Didi (China's Uber), and use the in-app chat to tell the driver exactly which "floor" or landmark you are near.
- [ ] **The Monorail**: The famous Liziba Station (where the train goes through a building) is on Line 2. It's the best way to get around and see the city.

## 3. The Viral Sights (And How to Actually See Them)

Chongqing is famous on social media. Here is how to experience the top sights without the frustration.

### Hongya Cave (洪崖洞)
This is the most famous spot. It looks like the spirit world from *Spirited Away*.
- **The Trick**: Do not try to enter from the ground floor — it will be packed with people. Walk to the **Qiansimen Bridge (千厮门大桥)** nearby to get the perfect photo.
- **Best Time**: After 7:30 PM when the lights turn on.

### Liziba Monorail Station (李子坝)
The train literally passes through a residential building.
- **The Trick**: Go to the designated viewing platform below the station. It's free and the best spot for photos.

### Drone Shows
Chongqing is famous for its massive drone light shows over the river.
- **The Trick**: Check local schedules (usually weekends). Watch from the **Jiangbeizui Riverside Park** on the opposite side of the river for the best view without the crowd.

## 4. Food: Beyond the Hotpot

You cannot leave Chongqing without eating. But be prepared for spice.

- **Chongqing Hotpot**: It's famous for being the spiciest in China. If you can't handle it, ask for **"Yuanyang Guodi"** (a divided pot with a non-spicy half).
- **Chongqing Noodles (小面)**: The city's breakfast staple. Cheap, spicy, and delicious.

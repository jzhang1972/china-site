Chongqing is unlike any other city in China. Known as the "8D Cyberpunk City," it's a place where monorails fly through buildings, streets are stacked on top of each other, and the night skyline looks like a scene from a sci-fi movie.

Viral videos have brought millions of tourists to this mountain city. But for a foreign traveler, Chongqing can also be confusing. Google Maps fails here, taxis can't find you, and cash is almost useless.

This guide covers what to set up before you go, how to navigate the vertical streets, and how to see the famous sights without the frustration.

---

## Before You Go: The Digital Essentials

Chongqing is a highly digital city. You need to prepare your apps **before** you arrive.

- [ ] **Set up Alipay**: Almost every shop, noodle stall, and taxi in Chongqing uses QR code payments. You cannot rely on cash here.
- [ ] **Get a China eSIM**: You'll need reliable internet for translation and navigation, and you'll be walking and climbing a lot.

## How to Navigate the "8D City"

In most cities, you walk on flat ground. In Chongqing, you might enter a building on the 1st floor and exit on the 15th floor onto a different street.

**The golden rule: do not use Google Maps.** It cannot handle the multi-level streets.

- [ ] **Use Amap (高德地图)**: It's the only map that understands Chongqing's vertical layout.
- [ ] **Use Didi for taxis**: Hailing a cab on the street is hard if you don't speak Chinese. Use Didi, and use the in-app chat to tell the driver exactly which floor or landmark you are near.
- [ ] **Take the monorail**: The famous Liziba Station (where the train goes through a building) is on Line 2. It's the best way to get around and see the city.

## The Viral Sights (And How to Actually See Them)

Chongqing is famous on social media. Here is how to experience the top sights without the frustration.

### Hongya Cave (洪崖洞)

This is the most famous spot. It looks like the spirit world from *Spirited Away*.

- **The trick**: Do not try to enter from the ground floor. It will be packed with people. Walk to **Qiansimen Bridge (千厮门大桥)** nearby to get the perfect photo.
- **Best time**: After 7:30 PM when the lights turn on.

### Liziba Monorail Station (李子坝)

The train literally passes through a residential building.

- **The trick**: Go to the designated viewing platform below the station. It's free and the best spot for photos.

### Drone Shows

Chongqing is famous for its massive drone light shows over the river.

- **The trick**: Check local schedules (usually weekends). Watch from **Jiangbeizui Riverside Park** on the opposite side of the river for the best view without the crowd.

## What to Eat (Beyond the Hotpot)

You cannot leave Chongqing without eating. But be prepared for spice.

- **Chongqing Hotpot**: It's famous for being the spiciest in China. If you can't handle it, ask for **"Yuanyang Guodi"** (a divided pot with a non-spicy half).
- **Chongqing Noodles (小面)**: The city's breakfast staple. Cheap, spicy, and delicious.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using Google Maps.** It cannot handle Chongqing's multi-level streets and will route you to the wrong floor. Install Amap before you fly.
- **Entering Hongya Cave from the ground floor.** It's the most crowded entrance. Walk to Qiansimen Bridge for the photo instead.
- **Arriving at Hongya Cave before sunset.** The lights turn on after 7:30 PM. Arrive earlier and you'll wait in a crowd for nothing.
- **Hailing a taxi on the street.** Drivers usually don't speak English and may not understand your destination. Use Didi and share your location in the app.
- **Relying on cash.** Many small vendors in Chongqing only accept QR code payments. Set up Alipay before you arrive.
- **Underestimating the walking.** Chongqing is built on hills and stairs. Wear comfortable shoes and plan fewer sights per day than you would in a flat city.

## FAQ: Chongqing Travel

### 1. How many days do I need in Chongqing?
Two to three days is enough for the main sights: Hongya Cave, Liziba, the monorail, and a riverfront walk. Add a day if you want to try the Ba Kingdom banquet or take a day trip.

### 2. Is Hongya Cave free to visit?
Yes, entry is free. The best photo spot is not inside the cave but on Qiansimen Bridge nearby, especially after 7:30 PM when the lights turn on.

### 3. Can I use Google Maps in Chongqing?
No. Google Maps cannot handle Chongqing's multi-level streets. Use Amap (高德地图), which has an English interface and understands the city's vertical layout.

### 4. What is the best way to get around Chongqing?
Use the monorail for sightseeing (Line 2 passes through Liziba Station) and Didi for short trips. Hailing a taxi on the street is difficult if you don't speak Chinese.

### 5. Is Chongqing safe for foreign tourists?
Yes. Chongqing is generally very safe, including at night. The main risks are practical: getting lost on the multi-level streets and struggling to pay without Alipay.

### 6. What should I eat in Chongqing if I can't handle spice?
Ask for "Yuanyang Guodi" (a divided hotpot with a non-spicy half). Chongqing noodles (小面) can also be ordered mild if you ask.

**Quick Answer:** The Li River karst landscape is what traditional Chinese ink paintings have depicted for over a thousand years, and it is printed on the back of the 20 yuan note. You have three ways to see it: the 4-hour cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo (¥200-500 per person), the Xingping bamboo raft (shorter, cheaper, better for the 20 yuan photo), or the Yangdi to Xingping hike (free, 3-4 hours). April to June and September to October are the best months.

## Why Is Guilin So Famous?

If you ask a Chinese person about Guilin, they will likely quote a famous saying: **"桂林山水甲天下" (Guilin's scenery is the best under heaven).**

But for a foreign traveler, Guilin might just look like a nice place with mountains. To truly appreciate it, you need to understand its place in Chinese culture. Guilin is not just a tourist destination. It is the physical embodiment of the Chinese aesthetic.

---

## The Real-Life Ink Painting

Have you ever seen a traditional Chinese ink painting (水墨画) of mountains fading into the mist? You might have assumed it was an idealized, stylized fantasy.

**It is not. It is Guilin.**

The karst topography of the Li River creates exactly the kind of layered, ethereal landscape that artists have been painting for centuries. The mist, the winding river, the vertical peaks. When you take a bamboo raft down the Li River, you are literally floating inside a classic Chinese painting.

---

## The 20 Yuan Note Secret

Here is a fun fact you can use to impress the locals: **look at the back of a 20 yuan RMB banknote.**

The image printed on it is **Yellow Cloth Shoal (黄布倒影)** on the Li River.

Taking a photo holding a 20 yuan note in front of the actual landscape is one of the most popular things to do in Guilin. The best spot for this photo is near Xingping, not on the main cruise. More on that below.

## Three Ways to See the Li River

Most guides only mention the cruise. There are actually three ways to see the same scenery, and they cost very different amounts.

### Option 1: The Guilin to Yangshuo Cruise

The classic option. A 4-hour cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo, passing the most famous karst peaks.

- **Cost:** Around ¥200-500 per person, depending on boat class.
- **Best for:** Travelers who want the full scenery in one trip and don't mind sitting for 4 hours.
- **Booking:** Book in advance on Trip.com. It is the easiest option for foreigners.
- **Downside:** It is the most crowded option, and the boat does not stop for the 20 yuan photo.

### Option 2: The Xingping Bamboo Raft

A shorter raft trip from Xingping to the Nine Horse Fresco Hill (九马画山) and back. This is the stretch printed on the 20 yuan note.

- **Cost:** Around ¥120-200 per person.
- **Best for:** Travelers who want the 20 yuan photo and a closer, quieter experience on the water.
- **Booking:** Buy tickets at the Xingping pier. No advance booking needed.
- **Downside:** It covers a shorter stretch of the river than the full cruise.

### Option 3: The Yangdi to Xingping Hike

A 3-4 hour walk along the river from Yangdi to Xingping, following the same karst scenery.

- **Cost:** Free (plus a small ferry crossing at Yangdi).
- **Best for:** Hikers and travelers who want to avoid crowds entirely.
- **Booking:** No booking needed. Start at Yangdi village.
- **Downside:** It is a real hike, not a stroll. Wear proper shoes and carry water.

## When to Go

Avoid the peak summer months (July-August) due to heavy rain and crowds. **April to June** (misty and green) and **September to October** (clear skies) are the best times.

The misty look that appears in the ink paintings is most common in spring. If you want the classic photo, go in April or May. If you want clear skies and easy hiking, go in September or October.

---

## Where to Stay: Guilin or Yangshuo?

Guilin city is the transport hub. Yangshuo, where the cruise ends, is the backpacker base with bamboo rafting, rock climbing, and nightlife. Most travelers stay in Yangshuo and use it as a base for the Li River and the surrounding countryside.

If you only have one day, stay in Guilin and take the cruise. If you have two or three days, stay in Yangshuo and add the Xingping raft or the Yangdi hike.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Booking only the cruise and skipping Xingping.** The 20 yuan note photo is taken near Xingping, not on the main cruise. If that photo matters to you, plan a separate stop.
- **Going in July or August.** Heavy rain, crowds, and the worst visibility of the year. April to June or September to October is a better choice.
- **Assuming the cruise stops for photos.** The main cruise runs on a schedule and does not stop at the 20 yuan spot. The bamboo raft from Xingping is better for that.
- **Underestimating the hike.** The Yangdi to Xingping walk is 3-4 hours on uneven ground, not a casual stroll. Wear proper shoes.
- **Staying only in Guilin city.** Most of the scenery and the better base is in Yangshuo, not Guilin city.
- **Not setting up Alipay before you go.** Raft tickets, snacks, and small vendors along the river often only accept QR code payments.

## FAQ: Guilin & Li River

### 1. What is the best way to see the Li River?
It depends on your priority. For the full scenery, take the Guilin to Yangshuo cruise. For the 20 yuan note photo, take the Xingping bamboo raft. For no crowds, hike from Yangdi to Xingping.

### 2. How much does the Li River cruise cost?
Around ¥200-500 per person, depending on boat class. The Xingping bamboo raft is cheaper, around ¥120-200 per person.

### 3. Where is the 20 yuan note photo taken?
Near Xingping, at Yellow Cloth Shoal (黄布倒影). The main cruise does not stop there, so plan a separate trip to Xingping if you want the photo.

### 4. Is Yangshuo better than Guilin?
Guilin city is the transport hub. Yangshuo is the better base for scenery, bamboo rafting, and nightlife. Most travelers stay in Yangshuo and use it as a base.

### 5. Do they speak English in Guilin?
English is limited. Download a translation app (Pleco or Google Translate offline) and use Didi for transportation.

### 6. When is the best time to visit Guilin?
April to June for the misty, ink-painting look. September to October for clear skies and easier hiking. Avoid July and August.

---

## Final Thoughts

Guilin is more than just a scenic spot. It is a piece of living history. When you float down the Li River, you are not just looking at mountains. You are stepping into the canvas that has inspired Chinese art for millennia.

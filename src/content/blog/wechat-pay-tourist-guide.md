WeChat Pay is China's other major payment app. If you already use WeChat to communicate with friends or colleagues in China, WeChat Pay is a convenient addition.

However, the setup is slightly more complex than Alipay. **Verification can take 2-3 days**, so start early.

This guide walks you through the entire setup process.

---

## WeChat Pay vs Alipay

| Feature | WeChat Pay | Alipay |
|---------|------------|--------|
| **Setup time** | 2-3 days | 10-30 minutes |
| **English interface** | ⚠️ Limited | ✅ Yes |
| **Foreign card support** | ⚠️ Good but slower | ✅ Excellent |
| **Where it works** | Everywhere | Everywhere |
| **Best for** | Messaging + payment | Primary payment app |

> ⏱️ **If you only have time to set up one app, choose Alipay. If you want a backup, set up WeChat Pay at least 3 days before your trip.**

---

## Step 1: Download WeChat

**iOS users:**
- Open the App Store
- Search "WeChat"
- Download the official app (green icon)

**Android users:**
- Open Google Play
- Search "WeChat"
- Download the official app

> 💡 WeChat is also available through some app stores in China, but the international version from Google Play/App Store works fine.

---

## Step 2: Register with Your Phone Number

1. Open WeChat
2. Tap **"Sign Up"**
3. Select your country code and enter your phone number
4. Tap **"Next"**
5. Enter the verification code sent via SMS
6. Set a password

> 📱 WeChat requires a phone number. Any international number works.

---

## Step 3: Activate WeChat Pay

1. Tap **"Me"** (bottom right corner)
2. Tap **"Services"**
3. Tap **"Wallet"**
4. Tap **"Bank Cards"**
5. Tap **"Add a Card"**
6. Enter your card details (card number, expiration date, CVV)

**Supported cards:**
- Visa
- Mastercard
- JCB
- Discover

> 💳 WeChat Pay supports fewer card types than Alipay. If your card doesn't work, try Alipay instead.

---

## Step 4: Verify Your Identity

This is where WeChat Pay differs from Alipay — verification is **slower**.

1. In Wallet → tap **"Identity Verification"**
2. Select **"Passport"** as your ID type
3. Upload a photo of your passport information page
4. Complete facial verification
5. **Wait 2-3 days for processing**

> ⏱️ **WeChat verification typically takes 2-3 days. If you plan to use WeChat Pay, start the setup at least a week before your trip.**

**Tips for facial verification:**
- Use a well-lit room
- Remove glasses
- Follow the on-screen instructions carefully

---

## Step 5: Start Paying!

Once verified, you're ready to go.

**To pay (merchant scans you):**
1. Open WeChat
2. Tap **"Me"** → **"Services"** → **"Wallet"** → **"Pay"**
3. Show your QR code to the merchant
4. The merchant scans it — payment complete

**To pay (you scan the merchant):**
1. Open WeChat
2. Tap **"Scan"** (top right in the main screen)
3. Scan the merchant's QR code
4. Enter the amount
5. Confirm — payment complete

---

## Fee Warning ⚠️

WeChat Pay charges a **3% international transaction fee** on transactions over 200 yuan (same as Alipay).

- **Small purchases**: fee-free
- **Large purchases**: 3% fee

## Troubleshooting

### "My card won't link"

- WeChat Pay supports fewer card types than Alipay
- Try a different card
- Check that your card supports 3D Secure
- If it still doesn't work, use Alipay instead

### "Verification taking too long"

- WeChat verification typically takes 2-3 days
- Contact WeChat support via the app
- Start the process at least a week before travel

### "I can't find Wallet"

- Make sure you've completed the full setup
- Tap "Me" → "Services" → "Wallet"
- If you don't see "Services," you may need to update the app

## FAQ

### Do I need WeChat to use WeChat Pay?

**Yes.** WeChat Pay is integrated into WeChat. You need the app.

### Can I use WeChat Pay without a Chinese bank account?

**Yes.** You can link an international credit card.

### Is WeChat Pay safe?

**Yes.** WeChat Pay uses bank-grade encryption.

### Why does WeChat Pay take 2-3 days to verify?

WeChat's verification process is manual, not automated like Alipay's. It requires human review of your passport and identity documents.
